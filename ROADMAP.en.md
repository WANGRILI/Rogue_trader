## North Star

RogueTrader is not designed to produce more text. Its long-term direction is an investment-decision infrastructure capable of independent research, adversarial reasoning, risk adjudication, execution expression, and measurable feedback.

```text
Research OS  →  Production Operations  →  Decision Integrity
             →  Execution Intelligence  →  Validation Loop
             →  Portfolio Intelligence
```

## Strategic Horizons

### Control Plane

Turn runtime modes, agent seats, model tiers, debate depth, and output protocols into composable and auditable controls.

### Simulation Loop

Connect parameterized plans, simulated orders, fill state, and portfolio equity so decision quality can be observed rather than assumed.

### Decision Intelligence

Develop cross-day strategy continuity, decision attribution, agent evaluation, and failure-mode analysis to reveal where the system is strong—and where it is blind.

### Portfolio System

Within explicit human authorization and strict safety boundaries, explore multi-asset capital allocation, unified risk budgets, and external execution adapters.

## Design Principles

- **Agentic by design**: role specialization, adversarial reasoning, and independent adjudication over single-call conclusions.
- **Evidence before action**: every execution expression must trace back to research evidence and a final decision.
- **Human accountable**: the system provides decision intelligence; people retain control of capital and execution.
- **Failure isolated**: analysis, delivery, notification, and future execution remain independently recoverable.
- **Audit everything**: versions, input boundaries, intermediate state, outcomes, and delivery are inspectable.
